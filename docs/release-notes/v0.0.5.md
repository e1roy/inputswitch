## New

- **Clear glass for the HUD (macOS 26 and later).** Settings → HUD → Glass now offers `Clear` alongside `Regular`.
- **Dimming.** Clear glass shows more of the wallpaper than regular glass, so it gets a slider (0–60%, 45% by default) that keeps the label readable over bright backdrops. Clear glass also draws its label in white.

Nothing changes unless you pick `Clear`: `Regular` stays the default, and older macOS keeps the material fallback.
